# Brand launch film

Source: article section 04 · Brand.

**What it does:** A 20-second launch film built from the real product site: assets, a five-beat story, synthesized music and three formats.

**Use it for:** Launch videos, product reels and ads. Keep one session per brand so the renderer and audio pipeline get reused.

**Placeholders:** `[PRODUCT]`, `[URL]`, `[METRIC]`, `[CTA]`

**Tips:** For a talking mascot, add a line like "the ElevenLabs key is ELEVENLABS_API_KEY in .env". Never paste a real key.

The prompt is in [`prompt.md`](prompt.md).
