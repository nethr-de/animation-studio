# Reference-driven style

Source: article section 05 · Reference.

**What it does:** Turns a reference video or frames into `docs/style_guide.md` and `docs/shotlist.md` before any code.

**Use it for:** Whenever you have a look to copy. Naming or showing a style works better than describing it.

**Placeholders:** `[DURATION]`, `[SUBJECT]`; put the reference in `./refs/`

**Tips:** Say what to take from it (palette, type, grain) and what not to (its subject). Let the model pick the technique.

The prompt is in [`prompt.md`](prompt.md).
